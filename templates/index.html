Introduction to PolyDB

About PolyDB

Polyamine conjugates refer to compounds that are covalently bonded between bioactive organic molecules with pharmacological properties and polyamine skeletons. These compounds exhibit both commonalities and diversities in their structural and functional characteristics. By harnessing the benefits of polyamines, these conjugates can regulate polyamine levels, enhance the liposolubility and targeting capabilities of bioactive organic molecules, thus holding significant potential for applications in anti-cancer and anti-inflammatory therapies.

The lack of scientific integration and comprehensive understanding of the shared features of polyamine conjugates has resulted in uncertainty and inefficiency in the design and synthesis of these molecules. PolyDB serves as a crucial resource to effectively manage and integrate relevant structural and functional information.
